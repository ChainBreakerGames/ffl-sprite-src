Privacy Policy – Food Fight Legends

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Food Fight Legends ("the game") is made by Chainbreaker Games.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet connection.

Your game progress

Your progress is saved only on your device. If you use the in-game "Save backup" code, that code is created on your device and only goes where you choose to paste or send it.

Your phone's own backup (Android backup) may copy the game's save to your Google account. That is controlled by your device settings and Google, not by us.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features such as ads or in-app purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released.

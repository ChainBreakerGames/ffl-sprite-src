Privacy Policy – Food Fight Legends

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Food Fight Legends ("the game") is made by Chainbreaker Games.

What we collect

Nothing personal. The game does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Online 1v1 matches

If you choose to play an Online 1v1 match, the game connects to our match server (hosted by Cloudflare) to pair you with another player. During the match it sends only gameplay data: your chosen hero, its power, a random player tag like "Chef#1234" made on your device, and your button presses. This data is passed to your opponent in real time and is not saved after the match. Like any internet service, the server briefly sees your connection's IP address to deliver the data; we do not store or use it. All of the rest of the game works offline.

Your game progress

Your progress is saved only on your device. If you use the in-game "Save backup" code, that code is created on your device and only goes where you choose to paste or send it.

Your phone's own backup (Android backup) may copy the game's save to your Google account. That is controlled by your device settings and Google, not by us.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Online matches use random player tags only; players cannot type names or chat.

Changes

If a future version adds features such as ads or in-app purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released.
